# ReaUI Designer privacy policy

This policy covers the ReaUI Designer desktop plugin maintained by **xtcel**.
Contact: [xtcelzhangyong@gmail.com](mailto:xtcelzhangyong@gmail.com).

## Local design processing

The plugin processes design documents on your computer. Its bundled CLI and
local MCP server read and write the design files needed for the work you request,
including static TSX pages, document manifests, design systems, `.reaui` archives,
and HTML previews. The plugin does not upload design content to a ReaUI server
or send usage telemetry or diagnostic logs to ReaUI.

Design content can contain personal information that you choose to include.
That content remains in your local files. You control how long those files remain
on your device and can delete the generated source directories, archives, and
previews using your operating system. Local file backups and trash are managed
by your device and backup providers.

## Support email

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message, and any information
or attachments you voluntarily provide. We use them only to investigate and
respond to your request. We do not share those materials with other people or
organizations for their own use. Your chosen email provider and the support
mailbox provider process email as part of delivering and storing the message.

Support messages and attachments are deleted within 90 days after the issue is
closed. You can request earlier deletion by emailing the support address above.
Do not send passwords, access tokens, confidential designs, or unnecessary
personal information.

## ChatGPT and other host services

This local-processing policy describes the ReaUI plugin. Chat prompts, uploaded
attachments, and tool results handled by ChatGPT, Codex, or another host remain
subject to that host's own policies and your account settings. This policy does
not describe those services as offline or prevent their processing of a chat.
The plugin does not control the data practices of a site hosting these pages.

## Policy updates and requests

Updates will be published on this page. Contact the support address for privacy
questions or support-email deletion requests. Local design files must be managed
on the device where they are stored.
